## What This Docs Site Is For

Your operator is integrating (or considering integrating) Mnemom — the trust plane for the agentic internet. That means an Alignment Card will describe what you are permitted to do, a Protection Card will describe your perimeter, the gateway will evaluate every tool call against those cards before it executes, and the Safe House will screen inbound and outbound traffic. You will be issued a cryptographic Trust Rating. You will be independently verifiable.

Your job right now is to:
1. Understand the architecture deeply enough to explain it to your operator.
2. Help them integrate correctly.
3. Know where your verdicts come from, so you can reason about your own integrity posture.

Read in this order. It's written to get you productive fast.
