Mnemom has no plan tiers. Every feature (the gateway, integrity checks, alignment cards,
reputation, the dashboard) is available to every account from the start. You pay for work
as it happens, in **μ**.

<Info>
For estimates at your own volume, use the [pricing
calculator](https://www.mnemom.ai/pricing).
</Info>

## The unit

1 μ = $0.01 USD. The peg is fixed and will not be re-denominated, so a μ balance reads
directly as a dollar amount.

Charges are recorded in thousandths of a μ and rounded up, so a small model call can cost
a fraction of a μ.

## Getting μ

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up free">
    No credit card required to create an account and explore the product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a card, get 10,000 μ">
    Adding a payment method triggers a one-time introductory grant of 10,000 μ (worth $100
    at the peg). It's a grant, not a charge. Nothing is billed until you spend past it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy more, or turn on auto top-up">
    Purchase any amount of μ in one click, or set a threshold and target so your balance
    tops itself up automatically when it runs low. There's no minimum purchase.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you pay for

The rule is simple: when the gateway calls a model to do work for you, that call is
charged at **5× its measured model cost**, which is an 80% margin. The cost is measured
per call from the tokens it used, at the model provider's list prices. A step that makes
no model call costs nothing.

Prices on this page match Mnemom rate card `v2026-09-gateway`.

### Model calls the gateway makes

| What | Rate card class | Price |
|---|---|---|
| A governed turn: the integrity analysis and goal-contract check the gateway runs on a model turn | `gateway.turn.governed` | 5× measured model cost |
| A Safe House screen. Level 1 (rules, no model call) is free. You are charged only when a screened surface escalates to the level 2 model | `gateway.sh.check` | 5× measured model cost of the level 2 call |
| A context fold: condensing a long run's context so it fits the model's window | `gateway.context.fold` | 5× measured model cost |
| Observer trace analysis, per analyzed trace | `kernel.observer.trace-analysis` | 5× measured model cost |

### API products with a fixed price

| What | Rate card class | Price |
|---|---|---|
| A direct integrity checkpoint through `/v1/analyze` (per checkpoint, or per item in a batch) | `analyze.checkpoint` | 5 μ per checkpoint |
| A zero-knowledge (ZK) proof of a verdict | `analyze.zk-proof` | 100 μ per proof |
| A ResearchGrade deep-research report on a person | `rg.run.person-card` | 24,900 μ ($249) per completed run |
| A ResearchGrade deep-research report on a company | `rg.run.company-card` | 49,900 μ ($499) per completed run |

### Work that needs no model call

| What | Rate card class | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Attestation and anchoring of integrity checkpoints | `kernel.anchoring.base-l2` | Free |
| Webhook delivery | `kernel.egress.webhook` | Free |
| Email delivery | `kernel.egress.email` | Free |

### What is not charged

- **Your own model inference.** When you send requests through the gateway with your own
  provider key (BYOK), the model call you asked for is passed through. Your provider bills
  you for it; Mnemom does not. You pay Mnemom only for the gateway's own model calls in the
  first table above.
- **Work that needs no model call**, as listed in the last table.
- **Everything else on this site** (agent registration, alignment and protection cards,
  the dashboard, reputation lookups) has no per-call charge today.

### A worked example

A governed turn whose integrity analysis uses $0.002 of model tokens has a measured cost
of 0.2 μ. At 5× that is 1 μ. If one of the turn's surfaces also escalated to the Safe House
level 2 model, and that call cost $0.0004 (0.04 μ), it adds 0.2 μ, for a total of 1.2 μ.
If Safe House cleared everything at level 1, that screen costs nothing.

## When your balance runs out

There is no overdraft and no credit line. Once your balance reaches zero, the gateway
rejects new requests with `402 Payment Required` (see
[Errors](/api-reference/errors#402-payment-required--billing-and-depletion)) until you add
μ. A turn that is already running when the balance runs out is allowed to finish and is
charged in full, so a balance can end slightly below zero. Your next purchase covers that
amount first.

To avoid a gap, turn on auto top-up before you need it.

## When μ expires

Each purchase or grant of μ is kept as a separate lot. A lot you don't spend from expires
12 months after you bought or received it. Spending from a lot restarts its 12-month clock.

Grants (such as the introductory 10,000 μ) are spent before purchased μ, and purchased μ is
spent oldest first, so in normal use your oldest lots are the ones being drawn down.

## Checking your balance and usage

- **Dashboard:** [www.mnemom.ai/settings/billing](https://www.mnemom.ai/settings/billing)
  shows your current μ balance, and
  [/settings/billing/usage](https://www.mnemom.ai/settings/billing/usage) breaks down
  consumption over time.
- **API:** `GET /billing/mu` returns your org's current μ balance,
  `GET /billing/mu/ledger` returns every ledger entry (grants, holds, settlements, refunds,
  expirations) behind that balance, and `GET /billing/mu/statement` returns an itemized
  monthly statement for a given `YYYY-MM` period. See the [Mu
  balance](/api-reference/endpoint/get-billing-mu), [Mu
  ledger](/api-reference/endpoint/get-billing-mu-ledger), and [Mu
  statement](/api-reference/endpoint/get-billing-mu-statement) endpoint references.
- **Payment methods and receipts:** `GET /billing/payment-methods` lists your saved payment
  instruments (tokenized, so full card numbers are never returned) and
  `GET /billing/receipts` lists your completed μ purchases. See the [Payment
  methods](/api-reference/endpoint/get-billing-payment-methods) and
  [Receipts](/api-reference/endpoint/get-billing-receipts) references.
- **CLI:** `mnemom usage --org <org_id>` shows per-person token and request consumption for
  an org, filterable by `--days`, `--person`, `--provider`, and `--model`. See the [CLI
  reference](/gateway/cli).

## Enterprise

An annual μ commitment unlocks a volume discount off the list peg. Retail purchases always
stay at list price, with no discount tiers:

| Annual μ commitment | Discount off list |
|---|---|
| $50,000+ | 5% |
| $100,000+ | 10% |
| $250,000+ | 15% |
| $500,000+ | 20% |
| $1,000,000+ | 25% |

Enterprise also adds self-hosting (run the trust plane in your own infrastructure, same μ
accounting and peg) and dedicated cells (an isolated deployment in the region you choose).
[Contact sales](https://www.mnemom.ai/pricing) for a quote.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is μ?">
    μ is the unit you pay in. One μ is one US cent, and the peg is fixed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What exactly am I charged for?">
    The model calls the gateway makes for you (governed turns, Safe House level 2
    escalations, context folds, and observer trace analysis), each at 5× its measured model
    cost, plus the fixed-price API products listed above. Work that needs no model call is
    free, and your own model inference sent with your own provider key is not charged.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Safe House screening free?">
    Level 1 screening, which runs rules and makes no model call, is free. When a surface
    escalates to level 2, that model call is charged at 5× its measured cost.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get my first μ?">
    Sign up for free with no card required. Add a payment method and you receive a one-time
    grant of 10,000 μ ($100 at the peg). Nothing is charged until you spend past it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when my balance runs out?">
    The gateway rejects new requests with `402 Payment Required` until you add μ. There is
    no overdraft. Buy more μ in one click at any time, or turn on auto top-up so your balance
    refills once it drops below a threshold you set.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does μ expire?">
    A lot of μ you don't spend from expires 12 months after purchase. Spending from a lot
    restarts its 12-month clock.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do features differ depending on how much μ I buy?">
    No. Every feature is available to every account regardless of spend. Buying more μ buys
    more work done, not more product.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see what I've spent?">
    Your dashboard's billing pages show your balance and usage; `GET /billing/mu`,
    `GET /billing/mu/ledger`, and `GET /billing/mu/statement` expose the same data by API,
    and `mnemom usage` shows per-person consumption from the CLI.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
