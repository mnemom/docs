Mnemom has no plan tiers. Every feature — the gateway, integrity checks, alignment cards,
reputation, the dashboard — is available to every account from the first minute. The only
thing you ever buy is **μ**.

<Info>
This page summarizes the model. For live figures, use the [pricing
calculator](https://www.mnemom.ai/pricing).
</Info>

## The peg

1 μ = $0.01 USD, and that peg is immutable — it will never be re-denominated. A μ balance is
a dollar figure you can read at a glance, and it never expires because of a plan you're on.

## Getting μ

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up free">
    No credit card required to create an account and explore the product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a card, get 10,000 μ">
    Adding a payment method triggers a one-time introductory grant of 10,000 μ (worth $100
    at the peg). It's a grant, not a charge — nothing is billed until you spend past it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy more, or turn on auto top-up">
    Purchase any amount of μ in one click, or set a threshold and target so your balance
    tops itself up automatically when it runs low. There's no minimum purchase.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checking your balance and usage

- **Dashboard:** [www.mnemom.ai/settings/billing](https://www.mnemom.ai/settings/billing)
  shows your current μ balance, and
  [/settings/billing/usage](https://www.mnemom.ai/settings/billing/usage) breaks down
  consumption over time.
- **API:** `GET /billing/mu` returns your org's current μ balance,
  `GET /billing/mu/ledger` returns every ledger entry (grants, holds, settlements, refunds)
  behind that balance, and `GET /billing/mu/statement` returns an itemized monthly statement
  for a given `YYYY-MM` period. See the [Mu balance](/api-reference/endpoint/get-billing-mu),
  [Mu ledger](/api-reference/endpoint/get-billing-mu-ledger), and [Mu
  statement](/api-reference/endpoint/get-billing-mu-statement) endpoint references.
- **Payment methods and receipts:** `GET /billing/payment-methods` lists your saved payment
  instruments (tokenized — full card numbers are never returned) and `GET /billing/receipts`
  lists your completed μ purchases. See the [Payment
  methods](/api-reference/endpoint/get-billing-payment-methods) and
  [Receipts](/api-reference/endpoint/get-billing-receipts) references.
- **CLI:** `mnemom usage --org <org_id>` shows per-person token and request consumption for
  an org, filterable by `--days`, `--person`, `--provider`, and `--model`. See the [CLI
  reference](/gateway/cli).

## Enterprise

An annual μ commitment unlocks a volume discount off the list peg — retail purchases always
stay at list price, with no discount tiers:

| Annual μ commitment | Discount off list |
|---|---|
| $50,000+ | 5% |
| $100,000+ | 10% |
| $250,000+ | 15% |
| $500,000+ | 20% |
| $1,000,000+ | 25% |

Enterprise also adds self-hosting (run the trust plane in your own infrastructure, same μ
accounting and peg) and dedicated cells (an isolated deployment in the region you choose).
[Contact sales](https://www.mnemom.ai/pricing) for a quote.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is μ?">
    μ is the unit of metered work on the trust plane. One μ is one cent, and the peg is
    immutable. A balance never expires because of a plan.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get my first μ?">
    Sign up for free with no card required. Add a payment method and you receive a one-time
    grant of 10,000 μ ($100 at the peg) — nothing is charged until you spend past it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when my balance runs low?">
    You can buy more μ in one click at any time, or turn on auto top-up so your balance
    refills automatically once it drops below a threshold you set.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do features differ depending on how much μ I buy?">
    No. Every feature is available to every account regardless of spend. Buying more μ buys
    more work done, not more product.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where can I see what I've spent?">
    Your dashboard's billing pages show your balance and usage; `GET /billing/mu`,
    `GET /billing/mu/ledger`, and `GET /billing/mu/statement` expose the same data by API,
    and `mnemom usage` shows per-person consumption from the CLI.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
